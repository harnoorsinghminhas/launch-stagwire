      The Stag Wire glossary

      Deal words, in plain English

      Every wire item uses these words the same way. Anyone can read them out loud.

      Seed round
      The first outside money a young company takes, usually to build a first version.
      Series A, B, C
      Later funding rounds, each usually bigger than the last. The letter is a label, not a grade.
      Valuation
      The price tag put on a whole company in a deal.
      Acquisition
      One company buys another: the acquirer buys the target.
      Acqui-hire
      A company bought mostly for its people; the product is often switched off.
      IPO
      Initial public offering: shares sold to the public on a stock market for the first time.
      Compute deal
      A deal for computing power, such as chips or data-centre time, rather than for a company.
      Reportedly
      Outlets carry it, but the companies have not confirmed it. A lead, not a fact.
